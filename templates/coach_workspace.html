VALUE PRINCIPLE

One exceptional, well-evidenced player-development outcome can be strategically more meaningful than many average outcomes. The platform therefore tracks quantity, outcome quality, player/context difficulty, attribution confidence and longitudinal durability separately before combining them into decision-support signals.
