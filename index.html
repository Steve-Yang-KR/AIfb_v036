v0.33 · Scientific Validation Layer

Measure the Measurement.

Before SSOT uses FCI, FSI, FEI or Super-Talent Development metrics for meaningful decisions, the platform should validate whether the measurements are repeatable, consistent across human coaches, stable across age/context differences, and predictive of observed improvement rather than merely descriptive.

Validation framework

Six scientific checks before trust

Each metric receives its own evidence-quality profile. A score can be visible while still carrying uncertainty and validation status.

1. Repeatability
Does the same player produce similar results under comparable conditions?
TEST
2. Inter-rater agreement
Do qualified coaches agree on the same observable behavior?
TEST
3. Age / context normalization
Are comparisons fair across age, position, drill and game context?
CALIBRATE
4. Construct validity
Does the metric actually represent the football behavior we claim?
VALIDATE
5. Longitudinal sensitivity
Can it detect meaningful development over weeks/months?
TRACK
6. Predictive usefulness
Does change in the metric relate to future observed development?
LATER

Interactive validation example

FCI · Scan-before-receive

Test whether scan-before-receive can be observed reliably across repeated sessions and coaches before using it in development recommendations.

Validation interpretation

Promising, not yet a universal benchmark.

Use this metric for within-player development tracking first. Avoid cross-player ranking until age, position, camera setup and drill-context normalization are validated.

Scientific rule: a low-confidence or poorly validated metric should not silently become a high-stakes recommendation. SSOT should expose confidence, provenance and validation status alongside the result.

v0.33 · AICL Scoring Lab

Measure AI-Augmented Coaching Without Replacing Human Coaching Judgment.

AICL is the validated AI-Augmented Coaching Level (L0–L9). AICS is the underlying 0–100 score. The model separates human coaching qualification from demonstrated AI-augmented capability and requires score, evidence volume, dimension floors and trust gates before a level is validated.

Validated level logic

Level = minimum of four gates.

1 · Score thresholdAICS determines the numerical capability band.
2 · Evidence thresholdPlayers, sessions and longitudinal duration limit validated level.
3 · Dimension floorHigh AI literacy cannot compensate for weak player impact or judgment.
4 · Trust gateMaterial safeguarding or integrity failure freezes/suspends progression.
Why this coach is L2, not L5

v0.33 · AICL Fairness Revision

Quality Determines Level. Quantity Determines Confidence and Breadth.

A part-time or specialist coach should not be held at a low AICL merely because they coach fewer players. Once minimum evidence eligibility is crossed, coaching capability, human judgment, player-development impact, evidence practice and trust determine AICL; sample size primarily determines how confidently and broadly that capability can be generalized.

Three independent professional signals

CAPABILITYAICL L5AICS 69
CONFIDENCEEC 68%Moderate
BREADTHB2Focused
Revised rule: AICL = capability subject to dimension floors, trust and minimum evidence eligibility. Evidence Confidence (EC) expresses certainty. Evidence Breadth (EB) expresses how widely the evidence generalizes.

Evidence Breadth · B0–B5

B0Insufficient
B1Initial
B2Focused
B3Established
B4Diverse
B5Extensive

Breadth considers players, sessions, duration, age groups, skill levels, positions and contexts — not player count alone. A focused specialist can therefore achieve a high AICL while honestly showing narrower breadth.

Revised L0–L9 Interpretation

Require breadth mainly when the claim itself becomes broader.

L1–L2 · Practice

Safe, competent AI-assisted workflow. Small but real minimum evidence is sufficient.

L3–L4 · Demonstrated

Visible player-development evidence and repeatable human judgment. Quantity has diminishing influence after eligibility.

L5–L6 · Advanced / Specialist Excellence

Part-time specialists can reach these levels through strong repeated outcomes, even with focused B2/B3 breadth.

L7 · Expert

Requires evidence that capability generalizes beyond a narrow player or context sample.

L8 · Leader

Adds institutional, mentoring or network contribution and broader validation.

L9 · Master

Exceptional sustained player development, methodology contribution, leadership and independent validation.

Minimum evidence still matters: one player and two sessions cannot establish advanced capability. But after a sensible eligibility threshold, player volume should show confidence/generalizability rather than function as a crude quality score.

v0.33 · Player Development Attribution Engine

Measure Player Improvement. Estimate What the Coach Actually Contributed.

Dimension D in AICL should not reward a coach simply for having talented players. The attribution engine separates observed development from expected development and then estimates the evidence-supported contribution associated with human coaching, AI augmentation and context. It is a provisional research model — not a causal claim engine.

Core attribution model

Observed ≠ Attributed.

Observed Development
− Expected Development without the intervention
= Development Above Expectation

Then estimate:
Human Coach + AI Augmentation + Player Effort + Environment + Unexplained
Scientific guardrail: SSOT should initially say “estimated contribution associated with the coaching cycle,” not “the coach caused X% of improvement.” Causal attribution requires stronger experimental or quasi-experimental validation.

Attribution hierarchy

Use the strongest comparator available.

Tier A · Controlled / quasi-experimentalStrongest research setting; matched controls or designed intervention.
Tier B · Matched longitudinal peersComparable age, baseline, role, training exposure and context.
Tier C · Within-player trajectoryCompare intervention period against stable prior development trend.
Tier D · Benchmark expectationPopulation benchmark adjusted for age/experience/context.
Tier E · Descriptive onlyNo defensible comparator: report improvement but do not attribute it.

v0.33 · Parental Consent & Guardian Portal

Protect the Player Before the Player Enters the Network.

For minor players, SSOT should make the parent or legal guardian the permission authority for registration, data use, media sharing, coaching relationships and opportunity visibility. The process begins with an adult guardian account, links the child to that account, captures granular consent, stores an auditable record, and supports renewal or withdrawal later.

Consent lifecycle

Adult Account → Child Link → Verify → Consent → Activate → Renew / Withdraw

The uploaded parental-consent brief calls for the parent to create the adult account first, add the child, electronically sign required authorizations, renew consent each season, and explicitly opt in where youth-safety training access is offered.

Turn the prototype into a deployable platform.

The browser prototype now has one persistent operational contract for identity/session, application records, uploads, consent, video jobs, payments, audit events, localization and analytics.
